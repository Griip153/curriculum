## How training works

- We meet **4 days a week**. The last week of every month is **Project Week**, ending in a **Showcase**.
- **Before** each session: watch the listed resources at home.
- **During**: short recap + Q&A, then everyone builds the Task of the Day.
- **After**: push your code and submit your **daily report** (see the `daily-reports` repo) before midnight.

> Golden rule: watching is not learning. You only know a concept when you have typed it, broken it, and fixed it yourself.
